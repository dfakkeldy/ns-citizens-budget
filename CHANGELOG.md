# Changelog

## v1.1 (unreleased)

Wording fixes prompted by a reader's notes on the printed copies. No dollar figure changes.

- **Own costs first, Competition (chapter 2):** honours the rights suppliers hold under signed contracts instead of "contracted options", notes Nova Scotia Health's commitment to re-tender when contracts reach their natural end, and asks it to check whether re-tendering would give better value before it extends or renews a contract.
- **Own costs first, Seafood (chapter 4):** adds that live lobster flies to Asia from Halifax Stanfield airport, so the Halifax and Sydney ports read as dual-use examples, not the seafood route. New source 65.
- **Planning for AI, ask 1:** a pilot tool is extended only if the staff time it saved over two quarters is worth more than its full cost: licences, usage charges and support, plus checking time the logs don't already count.

## v1.0, October 5, 2026: the submitted version

Emailed to budget@novascotia.ca on October 5, 2026, with both PDFs attached. The release PDFs are the exact files that were sent.

- **Own costs first** (41 pages): Plan A, Plan B, homes and growth, protections and 11 questions, with 64 sources.
- **Planning for AI** (8 pages): eight asks, a cost table and 8 questions, with 44 sources.
- **Review:** before release, the text and model were reviewed once. The main PDF went through eight Codex rounds and the companion through four, ending in approval.
- **Changes the review produced:**
  - Plan B no longer double-counts university and NSCC grants.
  - Home First's 2029-30 target fell from $15M to $5M.
  - The broader-sector 3% cut is attributed to the government's plan.
  - The companion's costs are shown as new spending outside both plans.
  - Its data-centre charge is disclosed as the one new charge.
