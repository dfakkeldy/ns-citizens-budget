At a glance

Make the plan real. Then get as close to balance as we honestly can.

Under the rules the government has set, the deficit cannot close quickly. So this submission has two tiers: a plan the government can adopt now, and a priced path toward balance that raises no taxes.

PLAN A Make the plan real
  • Names $85.5 million of the $590 million, government’s own costs first
  • Keeps $29 million of health savings in health to curb overruns
  • Publishes the $590 million by department, with a list of what will not be cut
$1.15B 2027-28 deficit, about the plan’s own figure. If this year’s overruns recur: $1.36 billion, against $1.38 billion on the plan.
PLAN B Closer to balance, no new taxes
  • Public-sector raises below plan, negotiated, never legislated
  • Slower doctor, drug and home-care cost growth through payment rules
  • Departments to 5% where room is left; nothing pushed onto towns
$560M 2029-30 deficit, against $862 million in the plan ($587 million with no department room left). At an assumed 2% wage path, zero would mean pay cuts.

Illustrative outcomes: both assume the government’s targets ($590 million, rising to $882 million by 2029-30) and every measure here are delivered in full, with no recurring overruns. The Bill 148 remedy is excluded.
