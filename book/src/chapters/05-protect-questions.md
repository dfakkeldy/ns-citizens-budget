# What I would protect, and what I would ask

## Not cut in either plan

- Front-line health care: nurses, doctors, emergency health services and rural emergency department hours.
- Classrooms, teachers and school food.
- Long-term care beds, home care and seniors' pharmacare.
- The Disability Support Program, supports for children with autism and other disabilities, income assistance, and the $165.2 million supportive housing and homelessness budget.
- Housing, public housing and rent supplements.
- The $53.6 million reinstated in March, with no further cuts to arts and culture grants.
- Transfers to municipalities.
- The Auditor General, the Ombudsman, the Human Rights Commission and the other independent officers.
- The information and privacy review office, with a proposed envelope of about $1.7 million, subject to workload costing. Its 2025-26 gross forecast was $1.72 million, including $0.40 million charged to other departments; its 2026-27 estimate is $1.45 million [10]. This is neutral only if Finance transfers equal funding now budgeted elsewhere.

These are conditions of both plans, to be published as the government's no-cut list. None of my measures closes a rural service point; any closure the government proposes should first publish a service-impact statement. My measures raise no tax or fee, legislate no wage restraint and need no layoffs, and every signed contract is honoured.

## Questions for Budget 2027-28

1. Is each Fiscal Stability Plan figure the total reduction counted that year, so that continuing 2026-27 measures count again in the $590.0 million? Will it be published by department and measure?
2. How large is the 2027-28 Restructuring Costs provision, and how does it split between wage settlements, workforce adjustment, legal settlements and other initiatives? What aggregate compensation increase does the plan assume?
3. Is the Bill 148 remedy provided for anywhere, how large is it, and does any part recur?
4. What asset produced the $80.0 million gain on disposal of Crown assets in 2026-27, and does 2027-28 assume another?
5. Does the 2027-28 plan include this year's Health and Seniors' care overruns, or will they appear later as additional appropriations?
6. What is the 2027-28 to 2029-30 capital plan by category, and when will net debt return below 40% of the economy?
7. Does the 2027-28 plan provide for the Yarmouth-Maine ferry, and at what amount?
8. How does the 2025-26 rental HST rebate forecast ($58.7 million) reconcile with the $14.9 million approved to March 2026, and how many extra units has the rebate produced?
9. What is Treasury's annual assessment of the Public Debt Management Fund: the spread between new borrowing and what the fund earns, and the liquidity floor it needs?
10. How much do pre-payment checks on physician claims recover each year, reported separately from other adjustments?
11. When does the Microsoft agreement renew, and does it allow seat reductions and exit?
