## Why the simulator number is small

The tool only lets departments move ±5%, Health can only go up, and restructuring, capital, health efficiencies and over-budget spending are not in it at all. The most anyone can cut in the tool is about $508M. The written submission (`book/own-costs-first-budget-2027-28.pdf`) carries everything the tool cannot show; send it too.

The tool's whole-point steps overstate the Invest NS trim (about $9.7M in the tool). The written submission's roughly $5M is the actual proposal; in the tool the difference goes to the Housing and Public Housing lines.
